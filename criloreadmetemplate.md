## 🌟 The Last Lead Generation Platform You'll Ever Need

Crilo transforms how solo web developers find, qualify, and convert local business leads. Instead of wasting hours on manual prospecting, Crilo **automatically curates localized business intelligence**, **audits their digital presence**, and **intelligently scores their buying intent** — delivering a pipeline of highly qualified leads ready for automated outreach.

Whether you're looking to scale your freelancing business, secure consistent monthly retainers, or simply want to harness the power of AI to unearth hidden market opportunities without hiring a sales team, Crilo is engineered to deliver results at an unprecedented scale.

### The Problem With Traditional Lead Gen for Solo Devs
- **Wearing Too Many Hats:** Juggling development, design, and outbound sales all by yourself is exhausting.
- **Wasted Time:** Endless hours spent manually searching Google Maps and directories instead of writing code.
- **Subjective Qualification:** Guessing which leads are actually worth contacting or have the budget for a real website.
- **The Cold Email Grind:** Manually writing bespoke, uninspired cold emails that get ignored because you don't have hard data to back up your pitch.

### The Crilo Advantage
Crilo unifies the entire workflow into a single, beautiful powerhouse:

```text

       └──────────────────────── Fully Automated ───────────────────────┘
```

## 💎 Core Capabilities

### 🧠 **AI-Powered Discovery & Scoring System**
Crilo doesn't just find businesses; it understands them. By tapping into Google's vast local data network and augmenting it with our sophisticated scoring engine, we separate the signal from the noise.
- **Multi-Factor Lead Scoring:** Algorithmically ranks leads based on digital footprint, market position, and tech-stack gaps.
- **Deep Website Analysis:** Automatically runs headless audits to check PageSpeed performance, Core Web Vitals, mobile responsiveness, and technical infrastructure.
- **Google Vertex AI Assistants:** Interact with intelligent agents that summarize business health and draft highly targeted outreach copy.

### 📈 **Advanced SEO Structure & Intelligence**
Built from the ground up to respect modern search engine requirements, both for our own platform and the sites we analyze.
- **Granular SEO Audits:** Instantly identifies missing meta tags, broken heading hierarchies, and missing structured data on prospect websites.
- **Platform SEO:** Out-of-the-box, Crilo itself leverages Next.js App Router for dynamic sitemaps, optimal indexing, and instantaneous page delivery.

### ✉️ **Automated Email Outreach & Campaigns**
Stop bouncing between a CRM and your inbox. Crilo handles the heavy lifting of personalized communication.
- **Hyper-Personalized Sequences:** Uses the intelligence gathered during the audit phase to inject real, irrefutable website performance metrics into your initial cold pitches.
- **Integrated Mail Engine:** Powered by Resend and SMTP engines, ensuring maximum deliverability.
- **Campaign Analytics:** Track open rates, engagement, and deal stage progression in real-time.

### 💳 **Frictionless Payment System & Monetization**
Designed for Product-Led Growth (PLG), Crilo includes an enterprise-grade billing structure.
- **Stripe Integration:** Seamless checkout, subscription management, and usage-based billing.
- **AI Token Economics:** Built-in rate limiting and token management for AI assistant requests, protecting infrastructure while providing tiered access.

### 🏢 **Client Contexts & Workspaces**
Work better and keep your diverse prospecting efforts organized. Crilo acts as your personal command center.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Define permissions if you ever decide to scale and bring on a VA or a contractor.
- **Workspace Contexts:** Seamlessly switch between different niche targets (e.g., local dentists vs. real estate agents) without losing state.
- **Shared Intelligence:** Drop notes, share audit reports, and collaborate on campaign sequences globally.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

Crilo is a testament to what is possible when modern web technologies are pushed to their absolute limits. It is designed to be blazingly fast, visually stunning, and infinitely scalable.

### **The Frontend Polish**
The presentation layer is meticulously crafted to evoke an emotional response.
- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router + Turbopack) for instant navigations and server-side rendering.
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 4 combined with `shadcn/ui` for a highly flexible, accessible, and beautiful design system.
- **Motion & Visualization:** `framer-motion`, `gsap`, and `react-three-fiber` create a living, breathing interface that wows users on every interaction.
- **State & Data Fetching:** `zustand` and `TanStack Query` ensure the UI is always responsive, optimistic, and snappy.

### **The Backend Engine**
Robust, secure, and ready for heavy compute.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL paired with **Prisma ORM** ensures type-safe, transactional reliability across millions of rows of lead data.
- **Authentication:** NextAuth v4 handles complex identity requirements including OAuth providers and secure credential flows.
- **Caching & Rate Limiting:** Upstash Redis is deployed at the edge to handle strict API rate limiting, ensuring our Google Places and PageSpeed integrations never exceed quotas.

### **The AI & Utility Layer**
- **AI Integration:** Google Vertex AI and Gemini models provide contextual deep learning capabilities.
- **Transactional Comms:** Custom templates rendered via `react-email` and dispatched flawlessly via Resend.
- **PDF/Image Pipelines:** Robust processing for generating visual proof of website audits to include in automated campaigns.
